# 🍽️ CraveCompass

### Find Your Food Feeling!

CraveCompass is a smart food recommendation website that helps users discover what to eat based on their mood, budget, and food preferences. Explore a variety of Indian dishes and find the perfect meal through a simple, interactive interface.

## ✨ Features

* 🍛 **Mood-Based Recommendations** — Discover food that matches your mood.
* 💰 **Budget-Friendly Choices** — Find meals within your preferred budget.
* 🥗 **Food Filters** — Explore vegetarian and non-vegetarian options.
* ⏰ **Serving-Time Availability** — View dishes based on their availability and serving hours.
* 📊 **Popularity Insights** — Explore popular food choices through visual analytics.
* ⚙️ **Menu Management** — Manage menu availability and serving hours.

## 🛠️ Built With

* HTML
* CSS
* JavaScript

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.
4. Explore CraveCompass and discover your next meal!

## 🎯 Project Goal

To make food discovery easier, more personalized, and enjoyable for everyone.
